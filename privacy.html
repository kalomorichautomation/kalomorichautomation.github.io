Privacy Policy

Kalomorich Uploader

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What this app is

Kalomorich Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by Kalomorich Productions. Its only purpose is to upload, schedule, and organize videos on the Kalomorich Productions YouTube channel, which is owned by the same person who operates the tool. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app uses

  • Google sign-in authorization: the channel owner grants the app permission to upload and manage videos and playlists on their own channel. The resulting access token is stored encrypted as a secret in the owner's private infrastructure and is used for nothing else.
  • Video files and metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails) created by Kalomorich Productions, which the app sends to YouTube.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information about viewers or any other users. It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscriptions, or analytics, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Information is sent only to YouTube, as needed to publish the channel's own videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately, and the stored authorization is then deleted.
